FMCG Order - Delivery & Returns Policy

1. Delivery Zones & Timelines
- Nairobi CBD & Industrial Area: Same-day delivery for orders placed before 10:00 AM. Orders placed after 10:00 AM are delivered the next business day.
- Machakos & Kiambu: Standard delivery is within 24 to 48 hours.
- Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): Free delivery requires a minimum order value of KES 10,000. Orders below this incur a KES 500 logistics fee.

2. Returns & Damages
- Damaged Goods: Any punctured packaging must be reported within 24 hours of receiving the goods. 
- Process: Customers must share a photo of the damaged batch. The delivery agent will collect the damaged stock on the next route.
- Refunds: We do not issue cash refunds. Approved returns are credited to the customer's Ledger Account for future orders.
